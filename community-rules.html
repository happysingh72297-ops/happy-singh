COMMUNITY RULES

COMMENTS KE LIYE NIYAM

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Yeh jagah sabke liye hai. Comment karne se pehle in niyamon ko padhein. Inhe todne par comment hat sakta hai aur user ko comment karne se block kiya ja sakta hai.

Allowed nahi hai

  • Gali-galoch aur apmaan: kisi ko gaaliyan dena, dhamki dena ya pareshan karna.
  • Hate speech: dharm, jaati, ling, desh, bhasha, rang ya viklangta ke naam par nafrat failana.
  • Spam: baar-baar ek hi comment, bekaar links, vigyapan ya "follow karo" jaise sandesh.
  • Jhooth aur afwah: jaan-boojh kar galat ya bhramak jaankari failana.
  • Nijee jaankari: kisi ka phone number, pata ya photo uski anumati ke bina daalna.
  • Hinsa aur gair-kanooni kaam: hinsa ko badhava dena ya gair-kanooni cheezon ko promote karna.
  • Adult ya aapatti-janak content.
  • Copyright wala content bina anumati ke.

Kya karein

  • Izzat se baat karein, asehmati ho to bhi.
  • Post ke vishay par hi comment karein.
  • Kuch galat dikhe to hume bataiye.

Karrwai

  1. Comment hata diya jaayega.
  2. Baar-baar ya gambhir ulanghan par user ko comment karne se block kar diya jaayega.

Hum har comment pehle se nahi padh sakte. Agar koi comment in niyamon ke khilaf lage, to hume zaroor batayein.
