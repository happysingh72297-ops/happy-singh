CONTENT KE ADHIKAR

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Hum doosron ke adhikaron ka samman karte hain aur chahte hain ki aap bhi hamare adhikaron ka samman karein.

1. Hamara content

Is website par hamari banayi hui posts, photos, text aur design par hamara copyright hai. Bina likhit anumati inki copy, repost, badlav ya vyavsayik istemal mana hai. Nijee, gair-vyavsayik share karte samay "Happy Singh" ko credit dena aur is website ka link dena theek hai.

2. Dusron ka content

Jahan hum kisi doosre ka content (photo, khabar, video) istemal karte hain, hum uska srot/credit likhne ki koshish karte hain. Content ke malik ke adhikar unke paas hi rahte hain.

3. Users ke comments

Comment mein doosron ka copyrighted content (jaise puri khabar, lyrics, photos) bina anumati na daalein.

4. Copyright shikayat ya removal request

Agar aapko lagta hai ki website par aapka content bina anumati istemal hua hai, to hume yeh jaankari bhejein:

  • Aapka naam aur sampark (email/phone).
  • Aapke original content ka vivaran ya link.
  • Website par jis jagah wo content hai, uska link (URL).
  • Ek kathan ki aap sachche vishwas se maante hain ki yeh istemal anumati ke bina hai.
  • Ek kathan ki di gayi jaankari sahi hai aur aap content ke malik ya unki taraf se kaam karne ke adhikrit hain.

Yeh sab Contact page ke form se bhejein. Sahi shikayat milne par hum content hata denge ya jaanch karenge.

5. Counter-notice

Agar aapka content galti se hataya gaya, to aap hume jawab bhej sakte hain, aur hum use dekhenge.

6. Baar-baar ulanghan

Baar-baar copyright todne wale users ko comment karne se block kiya ja sakta hai.
