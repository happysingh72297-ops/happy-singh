PRIVACY POLICY

AAPKA DATA KAISE ISTEMAL HOTA HAI

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Yeh Privacy Policy batati hai ki jab aap Happy Singh website istemal karte hain, to hum aapki kaun si jaankari lete hain, kyun lete hain, aur uska kya karte hain.

1. Hum kaun si jaankari lete hain

  • Guest ID: website kholte hi aapko ek anonymous (random) ID milti hai. Isme aapka naam ya email nahi hota.
  • Aapki activity: aapke comments (aapka likha hua naam aur text), likes aur unka samay, guest ID ke saath.
  • Contact form: agar aap sandesh bhejte hain, to aapka naam, sandesh aur aapka diya hua sampark (zaroori nahi). Ye sirf admin dekh sakta hai.
  • Technical jaankari: browser, device aur IP address jaisi basic jaankari, jo hamare service providers apne aap record karte hain.
  • Files: admin dwara upload ki gayi photos/files.

2. Hum jaankari ka istemal kyun karte hain

  • Website, likes aur comments chalane ke liye.
  • Comments aur likes dikhane ke liye.
  • Spam, galat istemal aur suraksha ke khatre rokne ke liye.
  • Website ko behtar banane ke liye.

3. Third-party services

Website in services par chalti hai, jo aapka data process kar sakti hain:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication aur Firestore database): guest ID aur comments/likes ka data.
  • Cloudinary: images aur files ki hosting.

In services ki apni privacy policies hain, jinhe padhna aapki zimmedari hai.

4. Data kitne samay tak rakha jaata hai

Comments aur likes tab tak rakhe jaate hain jab tak aap unhe delete karne ko nahi kehte ya hume unhe hatane ki zarurat nahi padti.

5. Hum aapka data bechte nahi

Hum aapka personal data kisi ko bechte nahi hain. Hum use sirf tab share karenge jab kanoon ke tahat zaroori ho.

6. Aapke adhikar

Aap apni jaankari dekhne, sudharne ya delete karwane ka anurodh kar sakte hain. Apna comment hatwane ke liye Contact page se sampark karein.

7. Bachchon ki privacy

Yeh website 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaan-boojh kar unka data nahi lete.

8. Suraksha

Hum aapka data surakshit rakhne ki koshish karte hain, lekin internet par koi bhi tareeka 100% surakshit nahi hota.

9. Badlav

Hum is policy ko badal sakte hain. Badlav is page par upar likhi "Last updated" tarikh ke saath dikhega.

10. Sampark

Sawal ya shikayat ke liye Contact page ka form istemal karein.
